RelaxDiff

Drop in two POSCARs. Get a relax sanity check, not a trajectory player. Below are four predefined cases — open any one for a full interactive report.

Case 1 · baseline

MgO normal relax

What a clean relaxation looks like. Light rattle, full symmetry preserved.
Case 2 · symmetry

SrTiO3 octahedral tilting

Cubic perovskite → tetragonal tilted variant. Symmetry breaking, bond angle changes.
Case 3 · alert

Si cell collapse

Overexpanded initial guess, MACE pulls it back. Large volume change, runaway atoms.
Case 4 · phase

TiO2 strained variant

Large lattice strain plus heavy rattle. Cell, bonds, and coordination all shift.
